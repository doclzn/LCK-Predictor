LCK PREDICTOR V9 — SCORELINE + UX REVIEW
=======================================

NEW QUESTION
------------
Beyond "who wins?", V9 estimates the exact BO3 score:
- Team A 2-0
- Team A 2-1
- Team B 2-1
- Team B 2-0

MODEL DESIGN
------------
The scoreline layer DOES NOT replace the central series probability.

Step 1:
Use the current central probability P(A wins series).

Step 2:
Estimate P(2-0 | winner) using:
- winner-oriented Elo difference
- winner-oriented last-5 series form
- winner-oriented last-10 series form

Step 3:
Decompose the central series probability:
P(A 2-0) = P(A wins) × P(sweep | A wins)
P(A 2-1) = P(A wins) × [1 - P(sweep | A wins)]
and equivalently for Team B.

This guarantees:
P(A 2-0) + P(A 2-1) = central P(A wins).
